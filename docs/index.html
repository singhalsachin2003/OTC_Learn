Before you install

Questions people ask

Is OTC Learn free?

Yes, and permanently. The thirty-six products the app shipped with — six asset classes, with their lessons, worked examples and question banks — are free with no account and no time limit. An optional subscription adds the asset classes released since, and anyone who installed the app before the subscription existed keeps those too.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Every lesson, worked example and question ships inside the app, so a session on a train behaves exactly like one at a desk. Nothing is fetched to read a lesson or answer a question.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up, no ads and no tracking, and progress is stored on the device. An optional account exists only so a new phone does not start you over, and it can be deleted from inside the app.

What does it cover?

Interest rate, FX, credit, equity and commodity derivatives, plus the market foundations all five run through — collateral, clearing and documentation — and, by subscription, exotics, risk and the Greeks, case studies and alternative underlyings. Each product has a five-step lesson, a worked example with real numbers, six key terms and a bank of twelve questions.

Is it financial advice?

No. It is educational content only — nothing in the app is advice, an offer to trade, or a recommendation to buy or sell any instrument.
