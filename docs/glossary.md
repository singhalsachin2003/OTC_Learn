# OTC derivatives glossary

358 key terms from the 66 products in OTC Learn, each linked to the product that teaches it. Where several products use a term, the first definition is shown and every product is listed.

## 0–9 {#letter-num}

### 25-delta {#25-delta}

The market’s usual reference point away from at-the-money, naming a strike by its delta rather than its rate so quotes stay comparable as spot moves.

In: [Risk Reversal](/OTC_Learn/product/fxrr/)

### 3-2-1 ratio {#3-2-1-ratio}

The standard bundle of three barrels of crude against two of gasoline and one of distillate.

In: [Crack Spread Swap](/OTC_Learn/product/crackspread/)

## A {#letter-a}

### Accrual factor {#accrual-factor}

The fraction of days in the period on which the reference fixed inside the range.

In: [Range Accrual Note](/OTC_Learn/product/rangeacc/)

### Accrual period {#accrual-period}

The window whose ex-dividend dates count — conventionally a single calendar year.

In: [Dividend Swap](/OTC_Learn/product/divswap/)

### Annual contract quantity {#annual-contract-quantity}

The reference volume for the contract year, around which the overall minimum and maximum takes are set.

In: [Swing Option](/OTC_Learn/product/swing/)

### Annualised basis {#annualised-basis}

The spread scaled to a yearly rate, which is the only form in which two contracts are comparable.

In: [Cash and Carry Basis](/OTC_Learn/product/cryptobasis/)

### Annuity factor {#annuity-factor}

The sum of the discount factors over the bond’s remaining life, which converts an upfront price difference into a running spread.

In: [Asset Swap](/OTC_Learn/product/assetswap/)

### Asian settlement {#asian-settlement}

Settlement against the average of the reference price over a period rather than its level on one date.

In: [Commodity Option](/OTC_Learn/product/cmopt/)

### Asset swap spread {#asset-swap-spread}

The running spread over the floating benchmark the package pays, and the cash market’s measure of the issuer’s credit risk.

In: [Asset Swap](/OTC_Learn/product/assetswap/)

### Attachment point {#attachment-point}

The loss level at which the bond begins to lose principal.

In: [Catastrophe Bond](/OTC_Learn/product/catbond/)

### Auction final price {#auction-final-price}

The single recovery price set by the industry auction, which every contract on that name settles against.

In: [Credit Default Swap](/OTC_Learn/product/cds/)

### Autocall trigger {#autocall-trigger}

The level at or above which the note redeems early on an observation date, usually the initial level.

In: [Autocallable Note](/OTC_Learn/product/autocall/)

### Averaging convention {#averaging-convention}

The rule turning a series of daily published prices into the single floating price for a period.

In: [Commodity Swap](/OTC_Learn/product/cmswap/)

## B {#letter-b}

### Backtesting exception {#backtesting-exception}

A day whose clean P&L loss exceeded the VaR the model predicted for it.

In: [Value at Risk](/OTC_Learn/product/valueatrisk/)

### Backwardation {#backwardation}

A curve where nearby prices exceed distant ones, which makes rolling a long position cost money.

In: [Metallgesellschaft, 1993](/OTC_Learn/product/metallges/)

### Barrier {#barrier}

A price level that brings an option into existence or extinguishes it if the underlying trades through it.

In: [OTC Equity Option](/OTC_Learn/product/eqopt/)

### Barrier shift {#barrier-shift}

A dealer’s practice of hedging to a level slightly beyond the contractual barrier to make the risk tradable.

In: [Barrier Option](/OTC_Learn/product/barrier/)

### Base currency {#base-currency}

The first currency in a pair — the one whose price is being quoted, so EUR/USD is dollars per euro.

In: [FX Forward](/OTC_Learn/product/fxfwd/)

### Baseload block {#baseload-block}

A constant delivery in every hour of the contract period — 1 MW for 31 days is 744 MWh.

In: [Power Derivatives](/OTC_Learn/product/power/)

### Basis {#basis}

The difference between a future’s price and the spot price of the underlying it references.

In: [Cash and Carry Basis](/OTC_Learn/product/cryptobasis/)

### Basis risk {#basis-risk}

The residual exposure left when the fixing differs from the rate the hedger transacts at onshore.

In: [Non-Deliverable Forward](/OTC_Learn/product/ndf/), [Commodity Swap](/OTC_Learn/product/cmswap/), [The LME nickel squeeze, 2022](/OTC_Learn/product/nickel/), [Longevity Swap](/OTC_Learn/product/longevity/)

### Basis spread {#basis-spread}

The basis points added to one floating leg so both legs are worth the same at inception, and the price at which the swap trades.

In: [Basis Swap](/OTC_Learn/product/basisswap/)

### Beneficial ownership {#beneficial-ownership}

Who is treated as holding a share for disclosure purposes — the dealer hedging a swap, not the fund receiving its return.

In: [Archegos, 2021](/OTC_Learn/product/archegos/)

### Bermudan swaption {#bermudan-swaption}

A swaption exercisable on any one of several agreed dates rather than on a single expiry date.

In: [Swaption](/OTC_Learn/product/swaption/)

### Block trade {#block-trade}

A trade above a published size threshold, permitted a short reporting delay so that publishing it does not itself move the market against whoever just dealt.

In: [Execution and Reporting](/OTC_Learn/product/execution/)

### Book value reporting {#book-value-reporting}

Reporting holdings at cost rather than market, which conceals a loss until it must be realised.

In: [Orange County, 1994](/OTC_Learn/product/orange/)

### Break-even move {#break-even-move}

The daily move at which re-hedging profit exactly covers the position’s theta.

In: [Delta and Gamma](/OTC_Learn/product/delta/)

### Breakeven inflation {#breakeven-inflation}

The fixed rate that makes both legs settle at the same amount, and so the market’s implied average inflation for that maturity.

In: [Inflation Swap](/OTC_Learn/product/infswap/)

### Bump and revalue {#bump-and-revalue}

Computing sensitivity by shifting an input, repricing the book and taking the difference.

In: [DV01 and Rate Risk](/OTC_Learn/product/dv01/)

### Buyer {#buyer}

The side that is the notional borrower, and so gains when the reference rate fixes above the contract rate.

In: [Forward Rate Agreement](/OTC_Learn/product/fra/)

## C {#letter-c}

### Calendar spread {#calendar-spread}

Long one delivery month and short another in the same commodity, trading the gap rather than the level.

In: [Amaranth, 2006](/OTC_Learn/product/amaranth/)

### Call {#call}

The right to buy at the strike, which caps what a consumer effectively pays for the commodity.

In: [Commodity Option](/OTC_Learn/product/cmopt/)

### Call spread replication {#call-spread-replication}

Hedging a digital with a long and a short vanilla struck either side of the trigger.

In: [Digital Option](/OTC_Learn/product/digital/)

### Cap {#cap}

A ceiling on the realised volatility used at settlement, limiting how much a seller can lose.

In: [Variance Swap](/OTC_Learn/product/varswap/)

### Caplet {#caplet}

One option within a cap, covering a single reset period of the underlying schedule.

In: [Cap and Floor](/OTC_Learn/product/capfloor/)

### Carry {#carry}

The funding, borrow and roll-down component of a day’s P&L, separate from market moves.

In: [P&L Attribution](/OTC_Learn/product/pnlexplain/)

### Carry trade {#carry-trade}

Borrowing in a low-yielding currency to invest in a higher one — profitable until the exchange rate moves.

In: [The Swiss franc floor, 2015](/OTC_Learn/product/chffloor/)

### Cash and carry {#cash-and-carry}

Buying the underlying and selling the future against it to capture the basis until expiry.

In: [Cash and Carry Basis](/OTC_Learn/product/cryptobasis/)

### Cash-or-nothing {#cash-or-nothing}

A digital paying a fixed cash amount if the condition is met, and nothing if it is not.

In: [Digital Option](/OTC_Learn/product/digital/)

### CDS-bond basis {#cds-bond-basis}

The CDS spread minus the asset swap spread on the same issuer and maturity, negative when the bond pays more than protection costs.

In: [Asset Swap](/OTC_Learn/product/assetswap/)

### Clearing member {#clearing-member}

A firm that faces the CCP directly; most market participants access clearing through one rather than joining themselves.

In: [Central Clearing](/OTC_Learn/product/clearing/)

### Client clearing {#client-clearing}

A clearing member facing the CCP on a client’s behalf, in a segregated account, with its own layer of margin and default provisions on top of the CCP’s.

In: [Central Clearing](/OTC_Learn/product/clearing/)

### Close-out netting {#close-out-netting}

Terminating every trade under a Master Agreement together on a default and reducing them to one net figure, rather than settling each on its own.

In: [The ISDA Architecture](/OTC_Learn/product/isda/)

### Collar {#collar}

A bought cap combined with a sold floor, which cuts the net premium in exchange for giving up gains below the floor.

In: [Cap and Floor](/OTC_Learn/product/capfloor/)

### Collateral {#collateral}

The high-quality assets the note proceeds are invested in and pledged against, in a vehicle-issued structure.

In: [Credit-Linked Note](/OTC_Learn/product/cln/)

### Collateral account {#collateral-account}

Where the note proceeds are held, so the vehicle can always pay and the investor takes no credit risk.

In: [Catastrophe Bond](/OTC_Learn/product/catbond/)

### Collateral buffer {#collateral-buffer}

The headroom a leveraged hedge holds so that a yield move produces a call it can meet without selling.

In: [The gilt LDI crisis, 2022](/OTC_Learn/product/ldi/)

### Collateral call {#collateral-call}

A repo lender’s demand for more security as the collateral falls, met by selling into the same fall.

In: [Orange County, 1994](/OTC_Learn/product/orange/)

### Confidence level {#confidence-level}

The quantile the measure is read at, commonly 97.5% or 99%.

In: [Value at Risk](/OTC_Learn/product/valueatrisk/)

### Confirmation {#confirmation}

Agreement of a trade’s terms with the counterparty — the check an invented trade cannot survive.

In: [Société Générale, 2008](/OTC_Learn/product/socgen/)

### Contango {#contango}

The opposite shape, where distant prices exceed nearby ones, and a long roll earns rather than costs.

In: [Metallgesellschaft, 1993](/OTC_Learn/product/metallges/)

### Contingent principal {#contingent-principal}

Repayment that is due in full only if no credit event occurs, and falls to recovery value if one does.

In: [Credit-Linked Note](/OTC_Learn/product/cln/)

### Continuation value {#continuation-value}

What the remaining allowance is worth if today’s is left unused — the figure a nomination decision is really judged against.

In: [Swing Option](/OTC_Learn/product/swing/)

### Contract price {#contract-price}

The price agreed today that the buyer will pay per unit on the delivery date.

In: [Commodity Forward](/OTC_Learn/product/cmfwd/)

### Contract rate {#contract-rate}

The rate agreed on the trade date, against which the reference rate is compared at fixing.

In: [Forward Rate Agreement](/OTC_Learn/product/fra/)

### Contract volume {#contract-volume}

The quantity the per-unit payoff is multiplied by to give the cash amount actually paid.

In: [Commodity Option](/OTC_Learn/product/cmopt/)

### Convergence {#convergence}

The narrowing of the basis to zero at expiry, which is what makes the captured spread a return.

In: [Cash and Carry Basis](/OTC_Learn/product/cryptobasis/)

### Convergence trade {#convergence-trade}

Buying the cheaper and selling the dearer of two near-identical instruments, expecting the gap to close.

In: [Long-Term Capital Management, 1998](/OTC_Learn/product/ltcm/)

### Convexity {#convexity}

The second-order effect that makes DV01 inaccurate for large moves in either direction.

In: [DV01 and Rate Risk](/OTC_Learn/product/dv01/)

### Cooling degree day {#cooling-degree-day}

The mirror image, counting degrees above the base, used for contracts written on summer cooling demand.

In: [Weather Swap](/OTC_Learn/product/weather/)

### Correlation risk {#correlation-risk}

Exposure to how likely defaults are to arrive together, carried by index tranche positions.

In: [CS01 and Jump to Default](/OTC_Learn/product/cs01/)

### Cost of carry {#cost-of-carry}

The storage, insurance and financing cost of holding a commodity until delivery, which shapes the forward price.

In: [Commodity Forward](/OTC_Learn/product/cmfwd/), [Cash and Carry Basis](/OTC_Learn/product/cryptobasis/)

### Coupon barrier {#coupon-barrier}

The lower level the underlying must hold on an observation date for a coupon to be paid without the note calling.

In: [Autocallable Note](/OTC_Learn/product/autocall/)

### Covered interest rate parity {#covered-interest-rate-parity}

The no-arbitrage relationship implying that the interest rate differential between two currencies should already be fully reflected in forward points, leaving no room for an extra spread.

In: [Cross-Currency Swap](/OTC_Learn/product/xccy/)

### Crack spread {#crack-spread}

The difference between the value of refined products and the cost of the crude used to make them.

In: [Crack Spread Swap](/OTC_Learn/product/crackspread/)

### Credit event {#credit-event}

The defined trigger — bankruptcy, failure to pay, restructuring and, for financials, governmental intervention — that terminates the contract.

In: [Credit Default Swap](/OTC_Learn/product/cds/)

### Cross-currency basis {#cross-currency-basis}

The spread added to one floating leg of a floating-for-floating cross-currency swap, which covered interest rate parity implies should be zero and, for the dollar since 2008, persistently is not.

In: [Cross-Currency Swap](/OTC_Learn/product/xccy/)

### Cross-currency basis swap {#cross-currency-basis-swap}

The floating-for-floating structure specifically, as distinct from the fixed-for-fixed and fixed-for-floating variants that also trade under the broader cross-currency swap name.

In: [Cross-Currency Swap](/OTC_Learn/product/xccy/)

### Cross-currency swap {#cross-currency-swap}

A longer-dated relative that also exchanges interest payments over its life, which an FX swap does not.

In: [FX Swap](/OTC_Learn/product/fxswap/)

### Crowded position {#crowded-position}

One whose size and holder are known to the market, so an unwind can be anticipated and traded against.

In: [Long-Term Capital Management, 1998](/OTC_Learn/product/ltcm/)

### CS01 {#cs01}

The change in value for a one basis point widening in the credit spread.

In: [CS01 and Jump to Default](/OTC_Learn/product/cs01/)

### Currency floor {#currency-floor}

A central bank commitment to prevent a rate falling below a level, held by intervening without limit.

In: [The Swiss franc floor, 2015](/OTC_Learn/product/chffloor/)

### CVA {#cva}

Credit valuation adjustment: the expected cost of the counterparty’s own default risk, subtracted from the textbook value.

In: [XVA and Counterparty Risk](/OTC_Learn/product/xva/)

## D {#letter-d}

### Daily contract quantity {#daily-contract-quantity}

The reference daily volume that the minimum and maximum daily nominations are expressed against.

In: [Swing Option](/OTC_Learn/product/swing/)

### Daily quantity {#daily-quantity}

The standard amount purchased on each fixing when the price is between the strike and the knock-out.

In: [Accumulator](/OTC_Learn/product/accum/)

### Day count convention {#day-count-convention}

The rule converting an annual rate into the fraction actually owed for a period, such as ACT/360.

In: [Interest Rate Swap](/OTC_Learn/product/irs/)

### Day-one P&L {#day-one-p-l}

Profit recognised when a trade is booked, on a model price rather than an observed one.

In: [P&L Attribution](/OTC_Learn/product/pnlexplain/)

### Days to liquidate {#days-to-liquidate}

How long a position would take to sell at normal volumes, and so how stale the price used to margin it is.

In: [Archegos, 2021](/OTC_Learn/product/archegos/)

### Decumulator {#decumulator}

The mirror structure, selling a daily quantity above spot and geared into a rally.

In: [Accumulator](/OTC_Learn/product/accum/)

### Default waterfall {#default-waterfall}

The strict order in which a CCP’s financial resources absorb a defaulting member’s losses, ending only after the defaulter’s own resources and the CCP’s own capital are exhausted.

In: [Central Clearing](/OTC_Learn/product/clearing/)

### Deliverable grade {#deliverable-grade}

The specification a contract can be settled with physically — and the thing a producer hedge must match.

In: [The LME nickel squeeze, 2022](/OTC_Learn/product/nickel/)

### Delivery date {#delivery-date}

The agreed date, or window, on which payment is made and the commodity is handed over.

In: [Commodity Forward](/OTC_Learn/product/cmfwd/)

### Delivery point {#delivery-point}

The named place where the goods change hands, which fixes who bears freight and where risk passes.

In: [Commodity Forward](/OTC_Learn/product/cmfwd/)

### Delta {#delta}

How much the option’s value changes for a small move in the spot rate.

In: [FX Option](/OTC_Learn/product/fxopt/), [Risk Reversal](/OTC_Learn/product/fxrr/), [OTC Equity Option](/OTC_Learn/product/eqopt/), [Delta and Gamma](/OTC_Learn/product/delta/)

### Delta exchange {#delta-exchange}

The offsetting index position traded alongside the option so the buyer starts without a directional view.

In: [Credit Index Option](/OTC_Learn/product/cdxopt/)

### Delta hedging {#delta-hedging}

Holding an offsetting position in the underlying so the option position has no directional exposure.

In: [Delta and Gamma](/OTC_Learn/product/delta/)

### Delta One {#delta-one}

Desks trading instruments that track their underlying one-for-one, such as index futures and swaps.

In: [Société Générale, 2008](/OTC_Learn/product/socgen/)

### Discount curve {#discount-curve}

The curve used to bring every cash flow on a trade, fixed and floating alike, back to a present value.

In: [Valuation and Marking](/OTC_Learn/product/marking/)

### Discounted settlement {#discounted-settlement}

The convention of paying the interest difference at the start of the period, reduced to its present value.

In: [Forward Rate Agreement](/OTC_Learn/product/fra/)

### Disorderly unwind {#disorderly-unwind}

A forced liquidation in which sellers compete, so the price achieved is far below the marked value.

In: [Archegos, 2021](/OTC_Learn/product/archegos/)

### Distillate {#distillate}

The middle-distillate product group covering diesel and heating oil, the third leg of the 3-2-1.

In: [Crack Spread Swap](/OTC_Learn/product/crackspread/)

### Dividend adjustment {#dividend-adjustment}

The agreed proportion of a declared dividend passed through on the equity leg, often net of withholding tax.

In: [Equity Swap](/OTC_Learn/product/eqswap/), [Contract for Difference](/OTC_Learn/product/cfd/)

### Dividend curve {#dividend-curve}

The strip of successive annual maturities, whose shape shows what the market will pay for each future year.

In: [Dividend Swap](/OTC_Learn/product/divswap/)

### Dividend notional {#dividend-notional}

The cash amount paid per point of difference between realised dividends and the strike.

In: [Dividend Swap](/OTC_Learn/product/divswap/)

### Dividend points {#dividend-points}

The contribution dividends make to an index level, the unit an index dividend swap is quoted and settled in.

In: [Dividend Swap](/OTC_Learn/product/divswap/)

### Doom loop {#doom-loop}

A self-reinforcing spiral where meeting a call requires selling the asset whose fall caused the call.

In: [The gilt LDI crisis, 2022](/OTC_Learn/product/ldi/)

### Dual range accrual {#dual-range-accrual}

A version conditioning the coupon on two references, paying only on days both are inside.

In: [Range Accrual Note](/OTC_Learn/product/rangeacc/)

### DV01 {#dv01}

The change in a position’s value for a one basis point parallel move in rates.

In: [DV01 and Rate Risk](/OTC_Learn/product/dv01/)

### DV01 (PV01) {#dv01-pv01}

The dollar change in a position’s value for a one basis point move in the relevant curve, the standard measure of interest rate sensitivity.

In: [Valuation and Marking](/OTC_Learn/product/marking/)

### DVA {#dva}

Debit valuation adjustment: the mirror-image value of the dealer’s own default risk, which a deteriorating dealer can — controversially — book as a gain.

In: [XVA and Counterparty Risk](/OTC_Learn/product/xva/)

## E {#letter-e}

### Effective duration {#effective-duration}

The true price sensitivity of a structured note to rates, which can be several times its stated maturity.

In: [Orange County, 1994](/OTC_Learn/product/orange/)

### Effective federal funds rate {#effective-federal-funds-rate}

The volume-weighted median rate on overnight unsecured borrowing in the US federal funds market, also published by the New York Fed.

In: [Basis Swap](/OTC_Learn/product/basisswap/)

### Embedded CDS {#embedded-cds}

The credit default swap inside the note, on which the investor is the protection seller.

In: [Credit-Linked Note](/OTC_Learn/product/cln/)

### Enhanced coupon {#enhanced-coupon}

The above-market interest the note pays, made up of the collateral return plus the protection premium.

In: [Credit-Linked Note](/OTC_Learn/product/cln/)

### Enhanced strike {#enhanced-strike}

The better-than-forward rate the client deals at, funded by the options it has implicitly sold.

In: [Target Redemption Forward](/OTC_Learn/product/tarf/)

### Equity leg {#equity-leg}

The side paying the return on the underlying share, basket or index over each period.

In: [Equity Swap](/OTC_Learn/product/eqswap/)

### Error account {#error-account}

An account for correcting mistaken bookings, and here the place concealed losses were parked.

In: [Barings, 1995](/OTC_Learn/product/barings/)

### Event of Default {#event-of-default}

A trigger generally caused by one party’s own conduct — non-payment, bankruptcy, a breach of the agreement — giving the other side the right, not the obligation, to terminate everything.

In: [The ISDA Architecture](/OTC_Learn/product/isda/)

### Exception handling {#exception-handling}

What happens after an alert fires, and the step that decides whether a control works at all.

In: [Société Générale, 2008](/OTC_Learn/product/socgen/)

### Exercise style {#exercise-style}

Whether the option may be exercised only at expiry (European) or at any time up to it (American).

In: [FX Option](/OTC_Learn/product/fxopt/)

### Exhaustion point {#exhaustion-point}

The loss level at which the bond’s principal is entirely gone.

In: [Catastrophe Bond](/OTC_Learn/product/catbond/)

### Expected shortfall {#expected-shortfall}

The average loss given that the threshold is breached — the mean of the tail rather than its edge.

In: [Value at Risk](/OTC_Learn/product/valueatrisk/)

### Explain {#explain}

The decomposition of a day’s P&L into the risk factors that produced it.

In: [P&L Attribution](/OTC_Learn/product/pnlexplain/)

## F {#letter-f}

### Family office {#family-office}

A firm managing a single family’s wealth, subject to lighter registration and disclosure than a fund.

In: [Archegos, 2021](/OTC_Learn/product/archegos/)

### Far leg {#far-leg}

The reversing exchange on the later value date, struck at the forward rate agreed at inception.

In: [FX Swap](/OTC_Learn/product/fxswap/)

### Fictitious trade {#fictitious-trade}

An invented offsetting position booked to make a real exposure appear hedged.

In: [Société Générale, 2008](/OTC_Learn/product/socgen/)

### Financial stability operation {#financial-stability-operation}

A central bank intervention aimed at restoring orderly markets rather than at setting monetary policy.

In: [The gilt LDI crisis, 2022](/OTC_Learn/product/ldi/)

### Financing leg {#financing-leg}

The floating payment — typically SOFR or €STR plus a spread — that buys the exposure.

In: [Total Return Swap](/OTC_Learn/product/trs/), [Equity Swap](/OTC_Learn/product/eqswap/)

### Fixed leg {#fixed-leg}

The side paying a rate agreed at inception that does not change for the life of the trade.

In: [Interest Rate Swap](/OTC_Learn/product/irs/), [Longevity Swap](/OTC_Learn/product/longevity/)

### Fixed price {#fixed-price}

The price agreed at inception for the whole life of the swap, quoted per unit of the commodity.

In: [Commodity Swap](/OTC_Learn/product/cmswap/)

### Fixing date {#fixing-date}

The day the reference rate is observed and the settlement amount is calculated.

In: [Forward Rate Agreement](/OTC_Learn/product/fra/), [Non-Deliverable Forward](/OTC_Learn/product/ndf/)

### Fixing rate {#fixing-rate}

The official reference rate published on the fixing date, against which the contract rate is compared.

In: [Non-Deliverable Forward](/OTC_Learn/product/ndf/)

### Fixing source {#fixing-source}

The named publisher of that rate, such as a central bank or an industry benchmark administrator.

In: [Non-Deliverable Forward](/OTC_Learn/product/ndf/)

### Flight to quality {#flight-to-quality}

A rush into the safest, most liquid assets, which widens every spread that pays for illiquidity at once.

In: [Long-Term Capital Management, 1998](/OTC_Learn/product/ltcm/)

### Floating leg {#floating-leg}

The side paying a rate that resets periodically against a published benchmark such as SOFR.

In: [Interest Rate Swap](/OTC_Learn/product/irs/), [Commodity Swap](/OTC_Learn/product/cmswap/), [Longevity Swap](/OTC_Learn/product/longevity/)

### Floorlet {#floorlet}

One option within a floor, covering a single reset period of the underlying schedule.

In: [Cap and Floor](/OTC_Learn/product/capfloor/)

### Forward points {#forward-points}

The adjustment added to or subtracted from spot to give the forward rate, driven by the interest rate differential.

In: [FX Forward](/OTC_Learn/product/fxfwd/)

### Forward sale {#forward-sale}

An agreement to deliver a quantity at an agreed price on a future date — the orthodox hedging instrument.

In: [Ashanti Goldfields, 1999](/OTC_Learn/product/ashanti/)

### Forward volatility {#forward-volatility}

The volatility expected between two future dates, which is what a cliquet is priced on.

In: [Cliquet Option](/OTC_Learn/product/cliquet/)

### Forward-starting option {#forward-starting-option}

An option whose strike is fixed at a future date rather than today, the building block of a cliquet.

In: [Cliquet Option](/OTC_Learn/product/cliquet/)

### FRA notation {#fra-notation}

The "3x6" form, giving the months from now to the start and to the end of the period covered.

In: [Forward Rate Agreement](/OTC_Learn/product/fra/)

### Freight index {#freight-index}

A daily published assessment of shipping rates, compiled from broker submissions, used as the settlement reference.

### Reverse Yankee {#reverse-yankee}

A bond issued by a US company in a foreign currency, most often euros, and swapped back into dollars — the mirror of a Yankee bond, in which a foreign issuer borrows dollars in the US market.

In: [Cross-Currency Swap](/OTC_Learn/product/xccy/)

### RFQ (request-for-quote) {#rfq-request-for-quote}

An execution method where a client asks a chosen number of dealers to compete for one specific trade, rather than trading against a continuous public order book.

In: [Execution and Reporting](/OTC_Learn/product/execution/)

### Risk reversal (quote) {#risk-reversal-quote}

The implied volatility of a call minus the implied volatility of a put at the same delta and tenor — the market’s standard shorthand for which side of the distribution carries the fatter tail.

In: [Risk Reversal](/OTC_Learn/product/fxrr/)

### Risk-theoretical P&L {#risk-theoretical-p-l}

The profit the risk model predicts from its own risk factors and sensitivities.

In: [P&L Attribution](/OTC_Learn/product/pnlexplain/)

### Risky annuity {#risky-annuity}

The duration-like factor that converts one basis point of index spread into an amount of cash.

In: [Credit Index Option](/OTC_Learn/product/cdxopt/), [CS01 and Jump to Default](/OTC_Learn/product/cs01/)

### Roll yield {#roll-yield}

The gain or loss from replacing an expiring contract with a later one, which accumulates every month.

In: [Metallgesellschaft, 1993](/OTC_Learn/product/metallges/)

### Route basis {#route-basis}

The gap between one ship’s actual economics and the index average the hedge references.

In: [Freight Forward Agreement](/OTC_Learn/product/ffa/)

## S {#letter-s}

### Schedule {#schedule}

The document that amends and elects within the Master Agreement’s standard form, adding bespoke terms and naming any Additional Termination Events specific to one relationship.

In: [The ISDA Architecture](/OTC_Learn/product/isda/)

### Seasonality {#seasonality}

The repeating within-year pattern in a price index, which largely cancels over whole years but must be modelled on short-dated and broken-dated trades.

In: [Inflation Swap](/OTC_Learn/product/infswap/)

### SEF / MTF / OTF {#sef-mtf-otf}

The regulated electronic venues — Swap Execution Facilities in the US, Multilateral and Organised Trading Facilities in the EU — that a sufficiently standardised derivative is required to trade on rather than being negotiated bilaterally.

In: [Execution and Reporting](/OTC_Learn/product/execution/)

### Segregation of duties {#segregation-of-duties}

Keeping trading, confirmation and settlement in separate hands so no one person can both trade and record.

In: [Barings, 1995](/OTC_Learn/product/barings/)

### Series {#series}

One vintage of the index, with a fixed constituent list and coupon, replaced by a new series at each roll.

In: [CDX Index](/OTC_Learn/product/cdx/)

### Settlement average {#settlement-average}

The mean of the index over the contract month, used instead of a single print so it cannot easily be moved.

In: [Freight Forward Agreement](/OTC_Learn/product/ffa/)

### Settlement currency {#settlement-currency}

The convertible currency the net difference is paid in, most often US dollars.

In: [Non-Deliverable Forward](/OTC_Learn/product/ndf/)

### Settlement period {#settlement-period}

The month or other window over which each leg’s index is observed and the spread is calculated.

In: [Crack Spread Swap](/OTC_Learn/product/crackspread/)

### Shape risk {#shape-risk}

The residual left when a flat hedge covers the same total energy as a profile that is not flat.

In: [Power Derivatives](/OTC_Learn/product/power/)

### Short squeeze {#short-squeeze}

A rise driven by shorts being forced to buy back into a market with too little offer to absorb them.

In: [The LME nickel squeeze, 2022](/OTC_Learn/product/nickel/)

### Short straddle {#short-straddle}

Selling a call and a put at the same strike: premium now, unbounded loss if the market moves far.

In: [Barings, 1995](/OTC_Learn/product/barings/)

### Single agreement {#single-agreement}

The status, stated in the Master Agreement itself, that every trade under it forms one contract with every other — the legal basis for close-out netting.

In: [The ISDA Architecture](/OTC_Learn/product/isda/)

### Skew {#skew}

The slope of implied volatility across strikes, quoted in the market as a risk reversal.

In: [Vega and the Volatility Surface](/OTC_Learn/product/vega/)

### Skin in the game {#skin-in-the-game}

The CCP’s own capital contribution to the waterfall, placed ahead of the mutualised guaranty fund so the CCP shares directly in the cost of a default it failed to price correctly.

In: [Central Clearing](/OTC_Learn/product/clearing/)

### SOFR {#sofr}

The Secured Overnight Financing Rate, an overnight rate derived from US Treasury repo transactions and published by the New York Fed.

In: [Basis Swap](/OTC_Learn/product/basisswap/)

### Sold call {#sold-call}

An option written away for premium, which gives up the upside on the very asset the seller produces.

In: [Ashanti Goldfields, 1999](/OTC_Learn/product/ashanti/)

### Spark spread {#spark-spread}

The power price less the fuel cost at a given heat rate — a gas generator’s gross margin.

In: [Power Derivatives](/OTC_Learn/product/power/)

### Special purpose vehicle {#special-purpose-vehicle}

A standalone issuing entity created for one deal, holding the collateral and the swap that make the note work.

In: [Credit-Linked Note](/OTC_Learn/product/cln/)

### Sponsor {#sponsor}

The insurer or corporate transferring the risk, and the party the bond pays when an event occurs.

In: [Catastrophe Bond](/OTC_Learn/product/catbond/)

### Spot rate {#spot-rate}

The rate for an exchange settling on the standard spot date, normally two business days after the trade.

In: [FX Forward](/OTC_Learn/product/fxfwd/)

### Spread {#spread}

The gap between the dealer’s buy and sell prices, paid on entry and exit of every trade.

In: [Contract for Difference](/OTC_Learn/product/cfd/)

### Spread margin {#spread-margin}

The reduced margin charged on offsetting legs, on the assumption that they move together.

In: [Amaranth, 2006](/OTC_Learn/product/amaranth/)

### Stack and roll {#stack-and-roll}

Holding the whole hedge in nearby contracts and rolling it forward, because distant months are not liquid enough.

In: [Metallgesellschaft, 1993](/OTC_Learn/product/metallges/)

### Standard coupon {#standard-coupon}

The fixed 100bp or 500bp running premium that standardised contracts pay instead of the traded spread.

In: [Credit Default Swap](/OTC_Learn/product/cds/)

### Standstill agreement {#standstill-agreement}

A negotiated pause in counterparties’ rights to call collateral or close out, bought here with equity.

In: [Ashanti Goldfields, 1999](/OTC_Learn/product/ashanti/)

### Stop-loss order {#stop-loss-order}

An instruction to trade at the market once a level trades — an order, not a guaranteed price.

In: [The Swiss franc floor, 2015](/OTC_Learn/product/chffloor/)

### Strike {#strike}

The rate above which a cap begins to pay out, or below which a floor does.

In: [Cap and Floor](/OTC_Learn/product/capfloor/), [FX Option](/OTC_Learn/product/fxopt/), [OTC Equity Option](/OTC_Learn/product/eqopt/), [Commodity Option](/OTC_Learn/product/cmopt/), [Accumulator](/OTC_Learn/product/accum/)

### Strike rate {#strike-rate}

The fixed rate on the swap the holder may enter, agreed when the swaption is traded.

In: [Swaption](/OTC_Learn/product/swaption/)

### Strike spread {#strike-spread}

The spread level, in basis points, at which the holder may enter the underlying index swap on exercise.

In: [Credit Index Option](/OTC_Learn/product/cdxopt/)

### Strip of FRAs {#strip-of-fras}

A run of FRAs covering consecutive periods, used where a single contract would leave later periods unhedged.

In: [Forward Rate Agreement](/OTC_Learn/product/fra/)

### Supervision {#supervision}

The obligation on a desk head to know what the desk holds, which no system replaces.

In: [Société Générale, 2008](/OTC_Learn/product/socgen/)

### Suppressed volatility {#suppressed-volatility}

Realised volatility held artificially low by intervention, which flatters every risk measure built on it.

In: [The Swiss franc floor, 2015](/OTC_Learn/product/chffloor/)

### Swap points {#swap-points}

The difference between the far and near rates, which is the price of the swap and reflects the interest differential.

In: [FX Swap](/OTC_Learn/product/fxswap/)

### Swing factor {#swing-factor}

The maximum daily nomination divided by the average daily volume, measuring how much flexibility the contract carries.

In: [Swing Option](/OTC_Learn/product/swing/)

### Synthetic credit portfolio {#synthetic-credit-portfolio}

A book of credit derivatives held for hedging or positioning rather than lending — here, indices and tranches.

In: [The London Whale, 2012](/OTC_Learn/product/whale/)

## T {#letter-t}

### Take-or-pay {#take-or-pay}

The obligation to pay for the annual minimum volume whether or not the buyer actually takes it.

In: [Swing Option](/OTC_Learn/product/swing/)

### Target {#target}

The cumulative gain that, once reached, redeems the structure and cancels all remaining fixings.

In: [Target Redemption Forward](/OTC_Learn/product/tarf/)

### Tenor {#tenor}

The total life of the swap, from effective date to maturity — commonly 2, 5 or 10 years.

In: [Interest Rate Swap](/OTC_Learn/product/irs/)

### Tenor basis {#tenor-basis}

The spread between two floating legs referencing the same benchmark over different periods, such as one month against three months.

In: [Basis Swap](/OTC_Learn/product/basisswap/)

### Term SOFR {#term-sofr}

A forward-looking rate for a period such as three months, derived from SOFR derivatives and therefore known at the start of the period.

In: [Basis Swap](/OTC_Learn/product/basisswap/)

### Termination Event {#termination-event}

A largely no-fault trigger — a change in tax law, illegality, a merger leaving a weaker surviving entity — that can end the relationship without either side having done anything wrong.

In: [The ISDA Architecture](/OTC_Learn/product/isda/)

### Theta {#theta}

The value lost per day from the passage of time, which is what pays for gamma.

In: [Delta and Gamma](/OTC_Learn/product/delta/)

### Threshold {#threshold}

The amount of uncollateralised exposure a CSA lets each side run before a margin call is triggered at all.

In: [Collateral and the CSA](/OTC_Learn/product/collateral/)

### Tick value {#tick-value}

The cash amount attached to each degree day of difference between the settlement index and the strike.

In: [Weather Swap](/OTC_Learn/product/weather/)

### Time charter average {#time-charter-average}

The average rate to hire a vessel class per day, the usual underlying for dry bulk contracts.

In: [Freight Forward Agreement](/OTC_Learn/product/ffa/)

### Tom-next {#tom-next}

A one-day swap rolling a position from tomorrow’s value date to the next business day.

In: [FX Swap](/OTC_Learn/product/fxswap/)

### Total return {#total-return}

An equity leg that passes on dividends as well as price changes, unlike a price return leg.

In: [Equity Swap](/OTC_Learn/product/eqswap/)

### Total return receiver {#total-return-receiver}

The side taking the asset’s income, gains and losses in exchange for paying the financing leg.

In: [Total Return Swap](/OTC_Learn/product/trs/)

### Trade cancellation {#trade-cancellation}

An exchange’s power to void executed trades, used here for a whole session’s nickel business.

In: [The LME nickel squeeze, 2022](/OTC_Learn/product/nickel/)

### Trade repository {#trade-repository}

The regulated entity every OTC derivative, cleared or not, has to be reported to — the systemic map of exposures that did not exist before 2008.

In: [Execution and Reporting](/OTC_Learn/product/execution/)

### Tranche {#tranche}

A slice of the basket’s losses between an attachment and a detachment point, sold as a separate contract.

In: [CDX Index](/OTC_Learn/product/cdx/), [The London Whale, 2012](/OTC_Learn/product/whale/)

## U {#letter-u}

### Unexplained P&L {#unexplained-p-l}

The residual between actual profit and what the risk factors account for.

In: [P&L Attribution](/OTC_Learn/product/pnlexplain/)

### Unwind cost {#unwind-cost}

The loss incurred while exiting a position, distinct from the loss the position already carried.

In: [Société Générale, 2008](/OTC_Learn/product/socgen/)

### Upfront payment {#upfront-payment}

The lump sum exchanged at inception that reconciles the fixed coupon with the level the name actually trades at.

In: [Credit Default Swap](/OTC_Learn/product/cds/)

### UTI / UPI {#uti-upi}

The Unique Transaction Identifier, generated once per trade so both counterparties’ reports can be matched, and the Unique Product Identifier, reused across every trade in the same instrument.

In: [Execution and Reporting](/OTC_Learn/product/execution/)

## V {#letter-v}

### Valuation date {#valuation-date}

A scheduled date on which the asset is remarked so accrued gains or losses are settled in cash.

In: [Total Return Swap](/OTC_Learn/product/trs/)

### Value at risk {#value-at-risk}

The loss not expected to be exceeded over a stated horizon at a stated confidence level.

In: [Value at Risk](/OTC_Learn/product/valueatrisk/)

### Value date {#value-date}

The date on which the two currencies are actually delivered to each other.

In: [FX Forward](/OTC_Learn/product/fxfwd/), [FX Swap](/OTC_Learn/product/fxswap/)

### Vanna {#vanna}

The cross sensitivity: how vega moves with the underlying, or delta with volatility.

In: [Vega and the Volatility Surface](/OTC_Learn/product/vega/)

### VaR model change {#var-model-change}

A revision to the risk model itself, which can reduce measured risk while the position is unchanged.

In: [The London Whale, 2012](/OTC_Learn/product/whale/)

### Variance notional {#variance-notional}

The cash amount paid per point of difference between realised variance and the strike.

In: [Variance Swap](/OTC_Learn/product/varswap/)

### Variance strike {#variance-strike}

The level settlement is measured against — the square of the volatility number the trade is quoted at.

In: [Variance Swap](/OTC_Learn/product/varswap/)

### Variation margin {#variation-margin}

The daily cash settlement of a derivative’s mark-to-market, owed whatever the physical position is worth.

In: [The LME nickel squeeze, 2022](/OTC_Learn/product/nickel/)

### Variation margin (VM) {#variation-margin-vm}

Collateral that settles the change in a portfolio’s mark-to-market, so neither side carries an unpaid gain or loss.

In: [Collateral and the CSA](/OTC_Learn/product/collateral/)

### Vega {#vega}

How much the option’s value moves when implied volatility changes by one point.

In: [OTC Equity Option](/OTC_Learn/product/eqopt/), [Vega and the Volatility Surface](/OTC_Learn/product/vega/)

### Vega notional {#vega-notional}

The approximate payoff per volatility point at the strike, equal to twice the strike times the variance notional.

In: [Variance Swap](/OTC_Learn/product/varswap/)

### Vessel class {#vessel-class}

A size category — Capesize, Panamax, Supramax — whose rates move together and define a contract.

In: [Freight Forward Agreement](/OTC_Learn/product/ffa/)

### Volatility skew {#volatility-skew}

The pattern of implied volatility across different strikes, which a flat single-number vol does not capture and a risk reversal is the standard way to measure.

In: [Risk Reversal](/OTC_Learn/product/fxrr/)

### Volatility surface {#volatility-surface}

Implied volatility mapped across every strike and every maturity for one underlying.

In: [Vega and the Volatility Surface](/OTC_Learn/product/vega/)

### Volga {#volga}

The sensitivity of vega to volatility itself — the convexity of the volatility exposure.

In: [Vega and the Volatility Surface](/OTC_Learn/product/vega/)

### Volumetric risk {#volumetric-risk}

Exposure to how much you sell rather than the price you sell it for, which a price hedge leaves untouched.

In: [Weather Swap](/OTC_Learn/product/weather/), [Power Derivatives](/OTC_Learn/product/power/)

### Voyage charter {#voyage-charter}

Hiring a vessel for a specific cargo and route, priced per tonne, as opposed to per day.

In: [Freight Forward Agreement](/OTC_Learn/product/ffa/)

## W {#letter-w}

### Washington Agreement {#washington-agreement}

The September 1999 accord in which European central banks capped gold sales, reversing a long price decline.

In: [Ashanti Goldfields, 1999](/OTC_Learn/product/ashanti/)

### Widow-maker {#widow-maker}

The March–April natural gas spread, across the end of the withdrawal season, and its reputation for violence.

In: [Amaranth, 2006](/OTC_Learn/product/amaranth/)

### Window forward {#window-forward}

A forward the client may settle on any date within an agreed range rather than one fixed date.

In: [FX Forward](/OTC_Learn/product/fxfwd/)

### Worst-of {#worst-of}

A basket convention under which every trigger and barrier test is run on the weakest of the underlyings.

In: [Autocallable Note](/OTC_Learn/product/autocall/)

### Wrong-way risk {#wrong-way-risk}

The case where a counterparty’s probability of default is itself correlated with the dealer’s exposure to it, so the two get worse together rather than independently.

In: [XVA and Counterparty Risk](/OTC_Learn/product/xva/)

## X {#letter-x}

### XVA {#xva}

The family of valuation adjustments — credit, funding, margin and others — added to a trade’s textbook value to price what a frictionless, default-free assumption leaves out.

In: [XVA and Counterparty Risk](/OTC_Learn/product/xva/)

## Y {#letter-y}

### Year-on-year inflation swap {#year-on-year-inflation-swap}

The periodic alternative, exchanging each year’s change in the index against a fixed rate on every payment date.

In: [Inflation Swap](/OTC_Learn/product/infswap/)

## Z {#letter-z}

### Z-spread {#z-spread}

The constant spread over the zero-coupon curve that makes a bond’s discounted cashflows equal its market price.

In: [Asset Swap](/OTC_Learn/product/assetswap/)

### Zero premium {#zero-premium}

A structure with no up-front cost, funded by options the client has sold rather than bought.

In: [Accumulator](/OTC_Learn/product/accum/)

### Zero-cost collar {#zero-cost-collar}

A risk reversal sized so the premium received on the sold option roughly offsets the premium paid on the bought one, for no net premium.

In: [Risk Reversal](/OTC_Learn/product/fxrr/)

### Zero-coupon inflation swap {#zero-coupon-inflation-swap}

The standard form, which exchanges nothing until maturity and then settles the whole compounded difference in one payment.

In: [Inflation Swap](/OTC_Learn/product/infswap/)
