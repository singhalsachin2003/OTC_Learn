# FX

Contracts used to hedge or speculate on currency exchange rate movements.

6 products, each with a five-step lesson, a worked example and a bank of twelve questions.

- [FX Forward](/OTC_Learn/product/fxfwd/) — Lock in a future exchange rate
- [FX Option](/OTC_Learn/product/fxopt/) — The right to exchange currency at a strike
- [FX Swap](/OTC_Learn/product/fxswap/) — Exchange currencies now and reverse it later
- [Non-Deliverable Forward](/OTC_Learn/product/ndf/) — A cash-settled forward for restricted currencies
- [Cross-Currency Swap](/OTC_Learn/product/xccy/) — Exchange principal and interest across two currencies
- [Risk Reversal](/OTC_Learn/product/fxrr/) — Sell an option to fund the one you actually want
