# Credit

Instruments that transfer or hedge the credit/default risk of borrowers.

6 products, each with a five-step lesson, a worked example and a bank of twelve questions.

- [Credit Default Swap](/OTC_Learn/product/cds/) — Insurance against a borrower defaulting
- [CDX Index](/OTC_Learn/product/cdx/) — A basket of CDS in one tradable index
- [Total Return Swap](/OTC_Learn/product/trs/) — Rent the full return of an asset
- [Credit-Linked Note](/OTC_Learn/product/cln/) — A bond whose repayment depends on a credit event
- [Asset Swap](/OTC_Learn/product/assetswap/) — Turn a fixed-rate bond into a floating one
- [Credit Index Option](/OTC_Learn/product/cdxopt/) — An option on a credit index spread
