# Interest Rate

Products that manage exposure to interest rate movements on loans, bonds and cash flows.

6 products, each with a five-step lesson, a worked example and a bank of twelve questions.

- [Interest Rate Swap](/OTC_Learn/product/irs/) — Trade fixed for floating payments
- [Swaption](/OTC_Learn/product/swaption/) — An option on an interest rate swap
- [Forward Rate Agreement](/OTC_Learn/product/fra/) — Lock a rate for one future period
- [Cap and Floor](/OTC_Learn/product/capfloor/) — Set a ceiling or floor on a floating rate
- [Inflation Swap](/OTC_Learn/product/infswap/) — Swap a fixed rate for realised inflation
- [Basis Swap](/OTC_Learn/product/basisswap/) — Swap one floating index for another
