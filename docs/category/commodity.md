# Commodity

Contracts that hedge or speculate on the price of physical commodities.

6 products, each with a five-step lesson, a worked example and a bank of twelve questions.

- [Commodity Swap](/OTC_Learn/product/cmswap/) — Fixed price for floating market price
- [Commodity Option](/OTC_Learn/product/cmopt/) — Cap or floor a commodity price
- [Commodity Forward](/OTC_Learn/product/cmfwd/) — Agree a price today for delivery later
- [Crack Spread Swap](/OTC_Learn/product/crackspread/) — Hedge the margin between crude and its products
- [Weather Swap](/OTC_Learn/product/weather/) — A payout driven by temperature, not price
- [Swing Option](/OTC_Learn/product/swing/) — The right to choose how much you take
