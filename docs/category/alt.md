# Alternative Underlyings

Contracts defined by what they reference rather than by their payoff — perpetuals and basis, freight, power, catastrophe risk and longevity.

6 products, each with a five-step lesson, a worked example and a bank of twelve questions.

Alternative Underlyings is part of the OTC Learn subscription; the 36 products the app shipped with are free.

- [Perpetual Swap](/OTC_Learn/product/perp/) — A future with no expiry, held to spot by a payment
- [Cash and Carry Basis](/OTC_Learn/product/cryptobasis/) — Buy the asset, sell the future, collect the difference
- [Freight Forward Agreement](/OTC_Learn/product/ffa/) — Hedging the cost of moving cargo, not the cargo
- [Power Derivatives](/OTC_Learn/product/power/) — A commodity that cannot be stored, priced by the hour
- [Catastrophe Bond](/OTC_Learn/product/catbond/) — A coupon that stops if the hurricane arrives
- [Longevity Swap](/OTC_Learn/product/longevity/) — Insuring against people living longer than planned
