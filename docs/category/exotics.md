# Exotics

Structured payoffs assembled from the vanilla products — barriers, digitals, accruals and the geared strips sold as zero-cost hedges.

6 products, each with a five-step lesson, a worked example and a bank of twelve questions.

Exotics is part of the OTC Learn subscription; the 36 products the app shipped with are free.

- [Digital Option](/OTC_Learn/product/digital/) — All or nothing, decided at one level
- [Barrier Option](/OTC_Learn/product/barrier/) — A vanilla that switches on or off at a level
- [Range Accrual Note](/OTC_Learn/product/rangeacc/) — A coupon that only earns on the days you are right
- [Accumulator](/OTC_Learn/product/accum/) — Buy at a discount daily, until it goes wrong
- [Target Redemption Forward](/OTC_Learn/product/tarf/) — A better rate that stops once you have won enough
- [Cliquet Option](/OTC_Learn/product/cliquet/) — A chain of options, each struck where the last one ended

[Get OTC Learn on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.otclearn.app)
