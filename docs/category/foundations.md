# Market Foundations

The infrastructure every other product runs through — collateral, clearing, valuation and the legal architecture that ties an OTC relationship together.

6 products, each with a five-step lesson, a worked example and a bank of twelve questions.

- [Collateral and the CSA](/OTC_Learn/product/collateral/) — The cash and bonds that back an OTC exposure
- [Central Clearing](/OTC_Learn/product/clearing/) — A central counterparty steps into every trade
- [Valuation and Marking](/OTC_Learn/product/marking/) — What a trade is worth, and which curve says so
- [The ISDA Architecture](/OTC_Learn/product/isda/) — The paperwork that makes every other product possible
- [XVA and Counterparty Risk](/OTC_Learn/product/xva/) — Why the same swap is never quite the same price twice
- [Execution and Reporting](/OTC_Learn/product/execution/) — How a trade gets done, and who has to know about it
