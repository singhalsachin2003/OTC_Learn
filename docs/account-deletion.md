# Deleting your OTC Learn account

**Last updated: 2 September 2026**

OTC Learn works with no account at all. If you have never signed in, there is no
account to delete and nothing of yours is stored anywhere but your own device —
see the [privacy policy](/OTC_Learn/privacy/) for what that covers.

If you did create an account, this page is how you get rid of it.

## How to request deletion

Email **<singhalsachin2003@gmail.com>** from the address you signed up with,
asking for your account to be deleted. Using the same address is what lets us
match the request to the account; if you cannot, say so and we will find another
way to confirm it is yours.

We will confirm by reply once it is done, normally within 30 days.

## What is deleted

Everything held against your account, permanently and with no archived copy:

- your email address and the account itself
- your mastery score for each product
- your review queue and question history
- your day streak and saved products

## What is not deleted, because we never had it

Purchases are handled by Google Play, not by us. A purchase is not tied to your
account — the app deliberately never tells RevenueCat who you are — so deleting
your account neither cancels a subscription nor forfeits a lifetime unlock. To
manage or cancel a subscription, use Google Play. For a refund, contact Google
Play.

We have never held your payment details at any point.

## Your copy on the device

Deleting the account removes the synced copy. The copy on your phone is yours
and stays until you remove it, either through **Profile → Reset all progress**
in the app, or by uninstalling.

## Deleting your data without deleting your account

If you want the synced data gone but would like to keep the account, say so in
the same email and we will do that instead.
